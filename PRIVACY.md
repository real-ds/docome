# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2024

## Overview

Docome is a desktop application for PDF and document processing. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

### Local Processing

Docome processes all documents **locally on your computer**. We do not upload, transmit, or store any of your documents on external servers.

- All PDF operations (merge, split, compress, etc.) happen locally
- Document conversion happens on your machine
- Signatures are created and applied locally

### No Cloud Storage

We do not provide cloud storage. Your documents remain entirely on your device.

### Optional Features

**OCR (Optical Character Recognition):**
- If you use OCR features, processing can use local engines (Tesseract/EasyOCR)
- No documents are sent to external OCR services unless you explicitly configure cloud-based OCR

## Data We Don't Collect

- We do not collect personal information
- We do not track usage analytics
- We do not collect device information
- We do not use cookies
- We do not have account registration

## Third-Party Libraries

Docome uses the following open-source libraries for document processing:

- PyMuPDF - PDF manipulation
- Pillow - Image processing
- python-docx - Word document handling
- openpyxl - Excel handling
- python-pptx - PowerPoint handling

These libraries process your documents locally as part of the application functionality.

## Security

- All processing happens locally
- No data leaves your device during normal use
- Password-protected PDFs use industry-standard encryption

## Changes to Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page.
