Merge, split, extract, remove, rotate, and compress pages with practical control over your documents.

Switch formats easily.

Convert PDFs to DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, Markdown, or plain text, with supported conversions back again.

Local-first by design

Your files stay yours.

Sensitive documents should not need to pass through another online converter. Docome is built for local processing, with a command-line interface for repeatable tasks.

 docome / terminal
$ docome pdf merge
  report.pdf appendix.pdf -o final.pdf
✓ Merged PDF saved to final.pdf
 
$ docome convert pdf2docx
  final.pdf -o final.docx
✓ DOCX saved to final.docx
